# Character bible

The cast of Pawtation. These are the owner's own characters; treat every "must-have" below as canon and never
swap in a different species, palette or marking. Everything here was read off the owner's reference art
(2026-11-15). The reference images themselves are other artists' commissions and are **not** stored in this
repo; this document and the rigs under `art/` are the working reference.

Billing order (how often each should be on screen): **Nova and Iggy** are the mascots and are there most of the
time, usually together. **Duskie** is a regular guest: rarer than the mascots, more common than anyone else.
The **neighbourhood** (Pickle, Vesper, Tuft, Hazel, Sprout, Stilts, Flurry, The Refills, and the first version's
Mochi and Biscuit) are rare cameo walk-ons.

All art in this project is SFW by default: characters are clothed and nothing is suggestive. See
`docs/SPEC_V2.md` for the opt-in After Dark content tier.

## Nova  (he/him) — serval, pharmacist, professional silly kitty

**Role:** mascot #1. Playful, smug in a harmless way, constantly a little bit hungry. Hospital pharmacist by day
(compounding, counting, "take with food"), gremlin by night. Cosmic motif: stars, sparkles, deep-space purples.
Partner: Iggy.

**Palette**
| Part | Hex |
|---|---|
| Fur, main (deep navy) | `#2b2d42` |
| Fur, shadow / ink line on white areas | `#1b1c30` |
| White markings (muzzle, bib, gloves, spots, tail bands) | `#f1eff8` |
| White, shaded | `#d9d6ea` |
| Inner ear, light | `#e9e7f7` |
| Inner ear, periwinkle patch | `#a9b4e6` |
| Iris | `#3d8fe0` (highlight ring `#7cc4ff`, lower shade `#1f6fc4`) |
| Nose | `#e9cfe2` |
| Paw pads, tongue | `#f29abf` / `#ee7fa6` |
| Tee, orange | `#e8894a` (shade `#cf7137`) |
| Smiley on tee | `#f7bd45` face, `#c98a1e` features |
| Name-plate magenta (his lettering colour) | `#f52fd2` |

**Must-haves (check every drawing against this list)**
1. **Ears:** enormous, tall, rounded-tip serval ears set high and close together, each roughly as tall as the
   whole head. Navy back and rim; inside is off-white with a soft periwinkle patch; two or three long fine
   hairs trail from the inner edge.
2. **Face:** navy "mask" over the top half; the lower face (muzzle, chin, lower cheeks) is white, and the
   boundary runs just under the eyes and flares into a pointed cheek tuft on each side.
3. **Forehead:** two short white vertical dashes, one above the inner corner of each eye (serval forehead
   stripes). A shorter third dash between them is allowed in large art.
4. **Freckles:** two or three tiny white dots at the outer corner of each eye.
5. **Eyes:** big, bright blue. His resting expression is half-lidded and pleased with himself. Small pale-pink
   nose, cat mouth (a soft "w"), long thin whiskers. Frequently has his tongue out (blep).
6. **Neck and chest:** navy neck with one to three short white horizontal dashes; a white heart-shaped bib on the
   chest that runs down the belly.
7. **Arms:** navy upper arm crossed by one white oval band; white forearms and hands (long gloves).
8. **Legs:** navy with white oval spots on hips and thighs, short white dashes on the shins, white toes.
9. **Tail:** long and slim, navy with jagged-edged white bands and a white tip.
10. **Build:** tall and lanky when drawn full-size; in chibi form he is all ears and head.

**Wardrobe and props**
- Default: orange T-shirt with a yellow smiley face.
- Pharmacist: short white coat over the tee, pen in pocket (for sync / "compounding" moments).
- Potion mode: brewing goggles pushed up on the forehead, a round flask of glowing pink-purple liquid.
- Sleepy: dark plum tee `#2e2440`.
- Signature prop: a grilled cheese sandwich (with a cheese pull), sometimes tomato soup.

**Voice:** playful, punny, lightly smug, affectionate. Sentence case, proper punctuation (the Rx Rounds Tracker's
Nova is the lowercase `:3` one; this Nova talks in full sentences and banters). Pharmacy shop-talk used for
jokes ("PRN naps", "take with food", "refills: unlimited"). Deadpan diagnosis: "It's toxoplasmosis." Loves grilled
cheese with tomato soup, tea, naps. Never mean. Calls Iggy "pup" when teasing.

## Iggy  (any/all pronouns; this project writes "they") — fennec fox, cook, camcorder gremlin

**Role:** mascot #2. Smaller than Nova and fluffier. Dry, warm, quick. The one actually holding the household
together, and the one filming Nova's worst ideas. Cooks ("Kiss the Cook" apron). Partner: Nova. In the owner's
other projects Iggy is "the puppy" (the Puppy–Kitty Non-Aggression Pact), which is a running joke, not a species.

**Must-haves**
1. **Ears:** very large fennec ears, wide at the base and more pointed than Nova's, angled outward. Tan front,
   periwinkle backs and tips, fluffy tufts at the base.
2. **Hair:** a messy, fluffy fringe that flops over the forehead, tan with periwinkle streaks. Big cheek ruffs.
3. **Glasses:** round, thin black frames. Always on. Non-negotiable.
4. **Eyes and face:** green eyes, dark nose, a small fang showing, short fox muzzle. Often a tiny tongue-out.
5. **Face marks:** three small periwinkle dots and a small diamond on the forehead; a short periwinkle mark
   under each eye.
6. **Body marks (periwinkle):** V-shaped lines at the collar, a heart outline on the left chest, three bands
   around each forearm, chevrons on the shins, a diamond at the navel (normally under the shirt).
7. **Tail:** big, bushy fox tail; the last third is periwinkle with a soft jagged edge; fine stripes near the base.
8. **Pendant:** a grey feather on a braided cord. Always on.
9. **Paws:** pink pads.
10. **Scale:** about three-quarters of Nova's height; in chibi scenes Iggy fits on Nova's head.

**Wardrobe and props**
- Default ("emo-casual", simplified from the owner's own notes): black tee with one periwinkle zigzag band
  across the chest, a striped wristband, pendant over the shirt.
- Cooking: "KISS THE COOK" apron over the tee, ladle or whisk; stands on a milk crate to reach the stove.
- Cozy: fur and markings only (chibi, featureless) for nap scenes.
- Filming: a small handheld camcorder.
- Optional big-art details: floral tattoo on the upper back, small wings-and-halo tattoo on the thigh.

**Voice:** deadpan, fond, a little chaotic underneath. Short sentences. Affectionately unimpressed by Nova.
Example register: "No. I am not going to 'spatchcock' you. Stop asking."

## Duskie  (he/him) — golden jackal, the permanent houseguest

**Role:** recurring guest (the household's third). Sunny, easygoing, turns up with snacks and stays for three
days. Golden-hour motif: sunsets, warm light. Close with both Nova and Iggy.

**Must-haves**
1. **Ears:** upright pointed jackal ears, clearly smaller than Nova's or Iggy's. Brown-gold backs and tips,
   cream inside with a little fluff.
2. **Face:** golden with a cream muzzle and throat; black nose; light blue eyes; an easy grin. A small

6. **Scale:** between Iggy and Nova in height; sturdier than either.

**Voice:** relaxed, warm, lightly teasing. Sunset and "golden hour" wordplay. Brings food. Says "hey, you two."

## How these versions differ from the owner's other projects

The owner asked for Pawtation's cast to look and behave unlike the versions in their other repositories
(surveyed 2026-11-15):

| Elsewhere | Here |
|---|---|
| Rx Rounds Tracker: chunky **pixel art** (ASCII-grid canvas, 32x37 and 16x20), hard 1 px black outline, lab coat and stethoscope, no body spots, Nova alone, lowercase `:3` voice, a corner helper that nudges about work | **Smooth vector sticker sprites** and **low-poly 3D renders**; full bodies with every canon marking; orange tee, no lab coat by default; Nova, Iggy and Duskie together; sentence-case banter between characters; they live on a "stage" that is part of the layout |
| Meow-core (PKNAP): flat outline-free **SVG busts**, eyes closed, static yin-yang; Iggy labelled "the puppy"; mock-bureaucratic voice | Awake, animated, full-figure; Iggy is unmistakably a fennec; warm household banter |
| Rounds / Meowbox: no character art (emoji, a robot-TV logo) | Characters drive the whole theme |

Standing rules carried over from the owner's notes: Nova is the owner's own character, so **no character ever
addresses the player with pet names** ("sweetie", "good boy" and the like); characters do not nag, sulk, decay
or keep streaks; reference art by other artists is never shipped.

## House style

Two looks, chosen by where the art is used (see `docs/ART_PIPELINE.md`):

1. **Die-cut sticker sprites (2D).** Chibi proportions (the head with ears is about half the figure), flat
   colours with one soft shade tone, a confident dark ink line, and a thick white die-cut border with a thin
   hot-pink edge and a soft drop shadow, like a vinyl sticker. Front-facing, friendly, readable at 96 px. Tiny
   four-point sparkles are the recurring decoration. Used wherever characters talk or react.
2. **Papercraft low-poly renders (3D).** Deliberately simple faceted models, flat-shaded, lit with a warm key
   light and a pink rim light, no textures; markings are coloured facets. Used for "hero" moments: the app
   icon, splash/about, the disc-spinning sync animation, posters and printable card labels.

Rules for both: keep each character's silhouette instantly recognisable (Nova = two tall rounded ears, Iggy =
two wide pointed ears + round glasses, Duskie = small pointed ears + bushy tail); use only the palettes above
plus the app's theme tokens; no gradients except the sticker shadow and background glows.
